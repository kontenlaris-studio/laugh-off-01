One line of office humor for the laugh-off: the shared microwave fish crime, escalated to a crime scene.

> "Someone microwaved fish at 9 a.m. again. The elevator now smells like a crime scene, and the suspect is still in the building."

126 characters — one fish, one elevator, one suspect still at large. The joke lands because the office treats a smell like a felony and the culprit like a fugitive.
